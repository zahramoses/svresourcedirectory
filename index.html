Good to know

Do I have to report to get help?

No. SACHA, the hospital care centres and the campus offices below can support you without a police or college report. Reporting is your decision, and you can choose to report later.

What happens at the hospital care centre?

You ask for the sexual assault nurse examiner (SANE) in the emergency department. The centre can provide immediate care, collect forensic evidence, take forensic photographs, help with safety planning, and connect you to follow-up care and counselling. Hospital staff do not report to the police without your permission. Charts for this care are kept separate from your other health records.

Is what I tell McMaster confidential?

McMaster protects confidentiality as far as its legal obligations allow. Information can be shared when it is needed to address a safety risk, such as risk of harm to yourself or others, or when the law requires it. Ask the office what its limits are before you share details. SACHA's support line is off campus and open at all hours.

Can I report to the police later?

Hamilton Police say there is no statute of limitations on sexual assault. Past, non-emergency assaults can be reported online, and a trained detective contacts you within 7 days. You can also call the non-emergency line or go to a station.
